To fix the inability to access the boot menu in Windows 11, force the system into **Advanced Startup** menu by holding **Shift** while clicking **Restart** from the **Start** Menu.
From there, navigate to **Troubleshoot > Advanced Options > UEFI Firmware Settings** to access BIOS/UEFI.

Additionally, the UEFI Firmware Settings will take you right to the **BIOS/UEFI Setup Utility** after pressing 'Restart'.
